# Privacy policy

Bence's Social Block does not collect, transmit or sell any data. It makes no network
requests at all: no server, no account, no analytics, no third-party code.

## What is stored, and where

Two things, both in your browser's local extension storage, on your own device:

- **Your settings.** The mode you picked for each site and the toggles you ticked.
- **A cached list of your YouTube subscriptions.** Channel names and links read from the
  YouTube sidebar. It exists so whitelist mode can allow your subscriptions on pages where
  the sidebar is not rendered - without the cache, an empty read would hide every channel
  you are subscribed to.

Neither is sent anywhere. Both are readable only by this extension.

## What is read from the pages you visit

On youtube.com and facebook.com the extension reads the page to find the things you asked
it to hide: channel names on video tiles, section headings, and the current path. This
happens in your browser and the results are not stored, apart from the subscription list
above.

When you open the popup it reads the address of the current tab once, so it can show the
settings for the site you are on. That address is not stored.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it has stored.
